06 / HOW IT WORKSCLARITY BEFORE COMMITMENT

Know what
happens next.

Every job is different, but a clear process helps both sides agree on the work before it begins.

01

Tell us about it

Share the issue, project type, general location and any useful dimensions or photographs.

02

Assess the site

Where needed, arrange a site visit to understand access, existing conditions, measurements and risks.

03

Receive a written scope

Clarify labour, materials, exclusions, estimated timing, payment stages and how changes will be handled.

04

Approve before work

Work begins after the scope, quotation, schedule and relevant approvals are agreed.

05

Track progress

Agree who provides updates, how questions are raised and how any variations are approved.

06

Inspect & hand over

Review the agreed work, note outstanding items and confirm handover arrangements.

    09 / QUESTIONSBEFORE YOU GET STARTED

    Good questions
    are part of good work.

    Is the quote form a confirmed quotation? ＋

    No. It prepares an enquiry for the business to review. A quotation should be based on the scope, site conditions, materials, labour, exclusions and any necessary specialist input. The customer should review and accept the quotation explicitly before work begins.

    Can you quote without visiting the site? ＋

    Some straightforward jobs may be discussed from photos and measurements, but hidden conditions, access, quantities or structural issues may require a site assessment. The business should explain any assessment fee before arranging it.

    Who buys the materials? ＋

    That should be agreed in writing before work starts. The scope should state which materials are included, who purchases them, the expected specification and how substitutions or price changes are approved.

    What happens if the work changes? ＋

    Pause and discuss the change. Agree its cost, effect on timing and any revised scope before proceeding, except where immediate action is needed to address an urgent safety risk.

    Do you handle electrical or plumbing work? ＋

    These are enquiry categories only. Any such work must be assessed and accepted only when the business can provide appropriately qualified personnel and comply with applicable requirements. Remove this service from the live site if it is not offered.

    Does this website store my photos or project details? ＋

    No. This front-end demonstration does not send form data to a server or upload files. It prepares a message in your selected app or displays text for copying. Any real storage, upload or privacy workflow requires a backend and a clear privacy notice.
